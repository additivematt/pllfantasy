How to Read This Data
  • MC Expected Value (Y-Axis): The mean fantasy points across 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations — what the model thinks a player will score on average. Higher = more valuable pick.
  • Risk Profile (Color): The standard deviation of simulated outcomes. Green = consistent floor player (low variance). Red = boom-or-bust (high variance). Use color to calibrate risk: in cash games favour green; in GPP tournaments favour red.
  • MC p90 Ceiling (Dot Size): The 90th percentile simulated outcome — the player's realistic ceiling. Bigger dots have higher upside. Pair with color: a big green dot is a safe star; a big red dot is a high-risk high-reward play.
  • Salary (X-Axis): Fantasy coin cost. The top-left quadrant (cheap, high EV) is where sleepers live.

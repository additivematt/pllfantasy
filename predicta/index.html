How to Read This Data
  • MC Expected Value (Y-Axis): The mean fantasy points across 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations — what the model thinks a player will score on average. Higher = more valuable pick.
  • Risk Profile (Color): The standard deviation of simulated outcomes. Green = consistent floor player (low variance). Red = boom-or-bust (high variance). Use color to calibrate risk: in cash games favour green; in GPP tournaments favour red.
  • MC p90 Ceiling (Dot Size): The 90th percentile simulated outcome — the player's realistic ceiling. Bigger dots have higher upside. Pair with color: a big green dot is a safe star; a big red dot is a high-risk high-reward play.
  • Salary (X-Axis): Fantasy coin cost. The top-left quadrant (cheap, high EV) is where sleepers live.
How Data is Calculated
  • XGBoost Classifier: Predicts the probability of a player hitting relative performance tiers: Boom (top 25% of position scorers in a week), Average (middle 50%), and Bust (bottom 25%). Features include matchups, rest advantage, rolling exponentially-weighted moving averages (EWMA), and an out-of-fold ceiling regressor meta-feature.
  • Bayesian Shrinkage: Matchup ratings are regularized to prevent overfitting on small sample sizes. Raw ratings are blended with a neutral prior of 1.0 using the formula: Ratingshrunk = α × Ratingraw + (1 - α) × 1.0, where α = min(1.0, ngames / 5).
  • Monte Carlo Simulations: Simulates 10,000 game-day trials per player by bootstrap-sampling historical scores, scaling by the opponent's defensive matchup multiplier (allowance ratio).
  • Gaussian Copula Correlation: Models interdependencies using Pearson correlation matrices (e.g., teammate positive correlations; zero-sum opponent faceoff/goalie negative correlations) to ensure roster variance mirrors reality.
  • Smooth Pool Blending (Pooling): Blends low-sample players' history with the position-wide pool. Draws are blended using α = min(1.0, ngames / 15) (where α is the chance of drawing from the player's history, and 1 - α is the chance of drawing from the position-wide pool) to stabilize projections.
